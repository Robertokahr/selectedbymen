This is the odd one out, on purpose, and the pubic hair trimmer with the most Amazon volume on the list — tens of thousands of reviews, around 4.2 stars. The Original is a short, palm-sized body with a replaceable ceramic T-blade, not a foil wand. Amazon listing B086W36F2W is the Onyx (matte black) color. Official kit: ceramic blade, two adjustable guides (3–6 mm and 9–12 mm), cleaning brush, USB charger. Meridian rates it waterproof and at 90 minutes of runtime.

Ceramic here means the blade sits a hair above the skin rather than a foil shave. Coarse, curly, and fine hair are all in the copy. It is small enough to throw in a weekender.

It is for travel, for people who nick themselves with foils, and for anyone who wants one tool from chest to groin without a six-inch handle. It is not for a close foil shave, and it is not a beard specialist even though it will buzz a jaw. Typical listing around $30 against a $50 list.

## Gillette Intimate i7

Gillette built this as a men’s intimate trimmer, not a full-body kit. Short handle, rubberized grip pads, LED Halo around the head, two clip-on combs (one to reduce cut risk, one at 2 mm), a 3-second travel lock, a charging stand, and about 100 minutes. Waterproof. Stainless blades. SkinFirst is Gillette’s name for closer-spaced, less pointy teeth on this line. The i7 listing is the smaller review sample of the five, with scores around 4.4.

It is for precision below the belt in bad light. If you want a short-handle ball trimmer with a light, this is the one — not a chest kit. It is not the pick if you also want a back handle, a dozen length settings, or a ceramic compact. Typical listing around $90 — the dearest of the five when it is not on promotion.
